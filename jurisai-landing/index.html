Not like a search bar.

01

Classify the question

Intent detection names the instrument: bail, sentencing, procedure, evidence, compliance. Wrong statute in, wrong authority out — so we classify before we retrieve.

Retrieve across engines

Semantic vectors and full-text inverted index fire in parallel; the fused rank list is re-ranked against the legal corpus, not web noise.
